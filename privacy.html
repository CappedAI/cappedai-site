Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

The short version: your conversations never leave your device.

CappedAI runs AI models locally on your own hardware. We don't operate a server that processes your chats, we don't have user accounts, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't sell data to anyone. The sections below explain the few times the app does touch the network, and exactly what happens when it does.

Who we are

CappedAI LLC, based in Austin, Texas. You can reach us at hello@cappedai.com. This policy covers the CappedAI apps and this website.

What stays on your device

All AI processing happens locally, on your device's own processor. Specifically, these never leave your device and are never transmitted to us:

  • Your prompts, messages, and the AI's responses
  • Your conversation history
  • Documents and files you open or import into the app
  • Notes you save to a knowledge pack's memory
  • Any content generated on device

We have no ability to read any of it. There is no CappedAI server that receives it, because we don't operate a backend at all.

When the app uses the network

The app is usable entirely offline. It connects to the internet only for the following, and never to process your conversations.

Downloading AI models

The app can download open-weight AI models so it has something to run. It first fetches a small public list of available models we publish on GitHub, then downloads the model file itself directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), the standard public host for open models. We link to models published there; the download is between your device and Hugging Face.

Downloading knowledge packs

Knowledge packs — topic-specific reference material and personas — are downloaded from our public releases on GitHub.

As with any download, the provider serving the file (Hugging Face or GitHub) will see the network request in the ordinary course of delivering it, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We don't receive that information. You can also skip downloads entirely and load your own model and pack files from local storage.

Connecting your own outside account

Some knowledge packs may offer to connect an account you already have elsewhere, so the assistant can answer questions using your own data from that service — for example, a trading-focused pack reading your own brokerage balances and positions.

Not active in the current release. No knowledge pack we currently publish uses this, so nothing in the app today can start an account connection.

When a pack does offer it, this is how it works:

  • It's optional and off by default. Nothing connects unless you explicitly choose to connect it, and the app works fully without it.
  • We never see your password. You sign in on the provider's own page. The app receives only an access token, which is stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Access is read-only. The app can look up information such as balances, positions, order history, and quotes. It cannot place, modify, or cancel trades, and it cannot move money. This limit is built into the app itself.
  • The data stays on your device. It travels from the provider directly to your device and is used on device to answer your question. It is never sent to CappedAI or shared with anyone else.
  • You can disconnect at any time, which removes the stored token from your device.

How we protect it

Keeping data on your device only helps if what's on the device is actually protected. Concretely:

  • The AI services run on your machine only. They're bound to your local loopback address, so they aren't reachable from your network or the internet.
  • They're authenticated with a key that changes every launch. Without this, a web page you happened to have open could quietly make requests to the AI running on your own machine. It can't.
  • Credentials for any account you connect go in your operating system's keychain — macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or the Linux secret service — not into a file on the drive.
  • Everything we download arrives over HTTPS, and account sign-in uses OAuth with PKCE, so the app never handles your password.

What this doesn't do. Files on the drive itself — your conversation history, documents, and saved notes — are not encrypted at rest today. Anyone with physical access to an unlocked drive, or software running on your computer under your own user account, can read them. If that's part of your threat model, keep the drive somewhere physically secure and use your operating system's own disk encryption on the computer you plug it into.

This website

If you enter your email address to join the early-access list, that address is sent to us through Web3Forms, a third-party form service, and we use it only to contact you about CappedAI. We don't sell or share it. Ask us at any time to remove you from the list and we will.

This site runs no analytics, no advertising trackers, and sets no tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, as essentially all web hosts do.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, and no sign-in to use the app
  • No analytics or telemetry in the app
  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers
  • No tracking you across apps or websites
  • No selling or renting your information to anyone
  • No training AI models on your conversations

Children

CappedAI isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Since the app has no accounts and collects no personal information, there's generally nothing for us to collect in the first place.

Your rights

Your app data lives on your device and is under your control — you can delete conversations, packs, models, and memory at any time from within the app, and deleting the app removes what remains. Because we hold no account data, there is typically nothing on our side to request or delete. The exception is your email address if you joined the early-access list; write to us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes to how the app handles your data will also be described in the app's release notes.
